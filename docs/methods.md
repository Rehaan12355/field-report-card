## Bare weeks

Cloud hides these fields for most of December and January: in 2024 the study
box had 1 clear satellite look in January and none in December. Yet 59% of the
area's heavy-rain weeks since 1991 fall in November to January.

So gaps are handled in two tiers:

- Gaps of up to 3 weeks are filled by drawing a straight line between the
  clear looks on either side.
- Longer gaps are bridged only when the last clear look before and the first
  one after agree. Both bare: the weeks between are "likely bare". Both green:
  "likely green". If they disagree, the weeks stay "unknown".

Likely weeks are an inference, not an observation. They are drawn differently
on the timeline and reported separately, for example "6 exposed weeks seen, 5
more likely". Snowy looks never count as bare.
